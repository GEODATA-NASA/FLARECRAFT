GeoFlare v1.0  -  Team FLARECRAFT (Assiut, Egypt)  -  NASA Space Apps 2026
Challenge: Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots

HOW TO RUN (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

  1. Double-click  GeoFlare.exe  (the Flick icon).
  2. Flick's window opens and loads GeoFlare (about 30 seconds).
  3. Flick asks "What do you want to open?": GeoFlare, the presentation, or the severity map.
  4. To stop, press "Stop GeoFlare" in Flick's window (or close the GeoFlare window in the taskbar).

  Nothing is installed. Everything GeoFlare needs is inside this folder.

IF WINDOWS SHOWS A BLUE "Windows protected your PC" BOX
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway". (The program is not signed, so Windows asks once.)
  Or double-click  "Run GeoFlare (backup).cmd"  instead: it does the same thing.

WHAT TO OPEN
  GeoFlare (story, Fires, Air) ...... http://127.0.0.1:8010/
  The presentation .................. http://127.0.0.1:8010/present/
  The classic severity map .......... http://127.0.0.1:8000/

INTERNET
  Works without internet: the fire history 2003-2026, the 2025 and 2026 archives, the AI severity model,
  the presentation.
  Needs internet: today's live fires, air quality, town search, and the map backgrounds.

ASK FLICK (optional)
  The chat needs a free Groq key (console.groq.com). Paste it into "Ask Flick key (optional).txt"
  and start GeoFlare again. Without a key, everything else works and the chat button is hidden.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  "Port 8000 (or 8010) is used by another program": close that program, or restart the computer.
  Anything else: the details are in app\logs.

THE DATA
  NASA FIRMS (MODIS and VIIRS active fires) and NASA POWER (weather). Fire history 2003-2025 is harmonized
  into VIIRS units; 2026 runs to 11 November (history) and 13 November (archive). Air quality and wind: Open-Meteo
  (Copernicus CAMS). Source code: github.com/GEODATA-NASA/FLARECRAFT
