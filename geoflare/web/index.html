      How it works

      NASA FIRMS active-fire records from MODIS (1 km pixels, 2000 onward) and VIIRS (375 m pixels, 2012 onward) are counted on a 0.25° grid each week, vegetation fires only. For years when both satellites flew, a gradient-boosting model learns how many VIIRS hotspots match what MODIS saw in each place and week, then rebuilds 2003–2011 in VIIRS units. Every year was also rebuilt while held out of training and compared with what VIIRS really saw; those errors set the uncertainty bands.
