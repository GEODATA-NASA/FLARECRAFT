Flick's voice on the stage.

Each .mp3 is one line he says, listed in lines.json (line -> file).
Recorded with Microsoft's neural voice en-US-AnaNeural (rate +6%, pitch +2Hz) through the open-source edge-tts tool.
To change a line or the voice, edit the line in scenes.js and re-run .tools/deck5/make_voice.py.
A line with no clip falls back to the browser's own speech voice.
